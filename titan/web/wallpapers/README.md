# Original Titan wallpapers

`coast.svg`, `alpine.svg`, and `valley.svg` are original SVG illustrations
authored for Titan's desktop redesign. Their mountain silhouettes, skies,
water, shoreline, gradients, and texture definitions were written directly
for this project. They contain no external images, fonts, linked resources,
or assets from UmbrelOS or TitanOS.

These files follow the license for original Titan material in the repository
root. Visual references informed the product direction, not the asset source.

## Fjord landscape

`fjord.png` is an AI-generated landscape created for Titan on 8 October 2026
with the built-in Imagegen tool. No input/reference image was supplied. The
generated PNG is included unchanged (1672 × 941 pixels); the three original SVG
wallpapers remain selectable. New browser profiles default to Fjord, while an
existing saved background choice is preserved.

This provenance record describes how the asset was created; it does not assert
exclusive rights or replace the third-party and commercial review described in
[LICENSING.md](../../../docs/LICENSING.md).

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original desktop wallpaper for Titan, a personal NAS desktop. Create a new original wide 16:9 high-resolution landscape photograph, ideally 2560x1440. A calm northern fjord at blue hour just before sunrise, seen at water level from a quiet rocky shore. Layered rounded and rugged mountains recede into fine atmospheric mist; a distant ridge catches a very restrained warm peach glow. The lower half is dark teal water with long delicate natural ripples and a few smooth rocks near an edge. Upper third: softly graduated slate-blue sky with thin clouds, calm and medium-dark, so white desktop greeting text will read clearly. Premium natural photography, believable fine rock and water texture, elegant soft light, muted sea-green, slate, pale warm horizon, quiet spacious composition. Keep the central third visually uncluttered for widgets and app icons. No people, buildings, boats, animals, text, logos, UI, borders or watermarks. Do not imitate or reproduce a known OS wallpaper or any reference photograph.
```

## Horizon (0.7.0)

`horizon.png` wurde am 9. Oktober 2026 mit dem eingebauten Imagegen-Werkzeug neu
erzeugt. Keine Referenzbilder, Quellgrafiken oder Assets aus TitanOS wurden an
das Werkzeug übergeben. `horizon.svg` ist die eigenständig gezeichnete
Vektorstudie. Beide Dateien liegen im Titan-Repository.

Verwendeter Prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original 16:9 desktop wallpaper for a personal NAS web interface, landscape 2560x1440 if possible. Create a new cinematic natural landscape: an immense still alpine lake at blue hour, layered ridges receding into lavender haze, a thin amber sunrise along the distant horizon, dark slate rock shores in the lower corners. Quiet, refined, plausible photographic texture, restrained color, natural light, no oversaturation. Broad uncluttered sky and mist in the upper central third for white greeting text and translucent widgets; darker smooth lake in the bottom third behind a small application dock. Full bleed. No text, no typography, no logos, no interface, no people, no buildings. This must be a new composition, not a reproduction of any existing operating system wallpaper.
